Support Pathway

Here's exactly what happens with the patterns in your check-ins and posts — and how to reach a real counselor whenever you decide you're ready.

How risk-scoring actually works

1

Signals are read locally

Mood trends and crisis-language cues are scored on-device or in your encrypted session — not tied to your name.

2

Only aggregate trends reach staff

Counseling staff see anonymized patterns, like "elevated stress signals this week in first-years," never an individual profile.

3

You decide if it becomes personal

If your own signals suggest you might want support, you'll get a private, gentle prompt — opting in is the only way staff learn who you are.

Ready to talk to someone?

Opting in shares your anonymous handle and recent check-ins with a licensed campus counselor, who will reach out privately within one business day.
